01 — About

A little bit about me

Get to know the person behind the code

I'm a 20-year-old Computer Science Engineering student with a deep curiosity for how things work — and a stronger drive to make them work better. Currently in my 4th year at VSB College of Engineering and Technical Campus, I've spent the last few years turning ideas into real, working software.

From a humble calculator to a fully functional library management system and a student welfare Android app, my projects reflect a love for clean architecture, thoughtful UX, and shipping things that genuinely help people.
